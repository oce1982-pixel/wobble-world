# Wobble World — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 September 2026_

Wobble World is a game for children. We designed it to collect **no personal information at all**.

**What we collect:** Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no ads, no analytics, and no in-app purchases.

**What is stored:** Your game progress (coins, Wobblers, outfits, room layout, scores and settings) is saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app (or clearing the site data in your browser) erases it.

**Network use:** The game works offline. The only network requests it makes are to load its own files and its display fonts (Google Fonts) when first opened. No game data is included in those requests.

**Children's privacy:** Because we collect no data, there is nothing to consent to, review or delete. The game complies with COPPA and GDPR-K by design.

**Changes:** If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new date.

**Contact:** via the project page, https://github.com/oce1982-pixel/wobble-world/issues

This policy is published at https://oce1982-pixel.github.io/wobble-world/privacy.html
