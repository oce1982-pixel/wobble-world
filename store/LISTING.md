## Description

Meet the Wobblers — bouncy, squishy little friends who live in your pocket!

🥚 **HATCH & COLLECT**
Crack open mystery eggs to find 48 unique Wobblers across Sky, Candy and Space Worlds, from Common cuties to Legendary rarities like Rainbow, Galaxy and Phoenix. Can you collect them all?

🎮 **EIGHT MINI-GAMES**
Catch falling stars, bop Wobblers as they pop up, match memory cards, copy the dance, race the colors, bounce to the sky in Wobble Jump, sprint through Wobble Run, and build the tallest Wobble Tower. Quick 30-second rounds — perfect for any moment.

👒 **DRESS UP YOUR BUDDY**
Party hats, wizard hats, sunglasses, capes, angel wings and more. Your buddy wears its outfit everywhere — even inside the games.

🛋️ **BUILD YOUR ROOM**
Decorate with sofas, rockets, pianos and castles. Pick your wallpaper and floor. Make it yours.

💖 **CARE & FRIENDSHIP**
Feed your buddy snacks, play together, and grow your friendship to five hearts. A happy buddy earns bonus coins!

🏆 **RANK UP**
Beat the rivals ladder in every game, earn 26 badges, level up to unlock new games, and keep your daily streak alive with the Lucky Spin.

✅ **MADE FOR KIDS**
No ads. No in-app purchases. No accounts. No chat. Nothing leaves the device. Works offline.
