Privacy Policy

Wobble World · last updated 18 September 2026

Wobble World is a game for children. We designed it to collect no personal information at all.

What we collect

No personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no ads and no analytics.

Optional purchases

The app-store version offers optional in-app purchases (a subscription, coin packs and egg bundles), placed behind a parental gate. Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google — we never see your payment details. To confirm what you bought, our purchase provider, RevenueCat, receives an anonymous random app ID and the store's purchase receipt. That ID is not linked to your name, email or device identifiers, and it is used only to restore purchases. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What is stored

Your game progress (coins, Wobblers, outfits, room layout, scores and settings) is saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app, or clearing the site data in your browser, erases it.

Network use

The game works offline. The only network requests it makes are to load its own files and its display fonts (Google Fonts) when first opened, and, in the app-store version, to validate purchases as described above. No game data is included in those requests.

Children's privacy

We collect no personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases require an adult to pass a parental gate. The game is designed to comply with COPPA and GDPR-K.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the game's project page: github.com/oce1982-pixel/wobble-world.
