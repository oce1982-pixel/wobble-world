Terms of Use

Wobble World · last updated 18 September 2026

The game

Wobble World is free to play. Everything in the game can be earned by playing. Optional purchases are available for players who want to speed things up or support the game.

Purchases

Purchases are made through the Apple App Store or Google Play using the payment method on that account, and are subject to that store's terms. A parental gate appears before every purchase; purchases are intended to be made by a parent or guardian.

Wobble Pass is a monthly subscription. It renews automatically each month until cancelled. Cancel at any time in your device's subscription settings (iPhone: Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions; Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions). Cancelling stops future charges; benefits continue until the end of the paid period.

Coin packs and Golden Eggs are added to your game immediately after purchase and are not refundable once used. All Worlds Pass is a one-time purchase.

Refund requests are handled by Apple or Google under their policies. Use Restore in the Extras tab of the Shop to recover purchases on a new device (same store account required).

Progress

Game progress is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes progress; we cannot restore it. Purchases tied to your store account (Wobble Pass, All Worlds Pass) can be restored; coins and eggs already delivered cannot.

Fair play

Please don't tamper with the game's storage or purchase system. We may change the game, its items or prices over time.
